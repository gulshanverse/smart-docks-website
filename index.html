How SmartDocs thinks

Simple on the outside.

01

Start with the goal

People should not need to know which technical tool they need. The workspace will begin with what they are trying to accomplish.

02

Use the right engine

Deterministic transformations should stay deterministic. AI will be reserved for understanding, planning, and document intelligence.

03

Show the proof

A result is not complete until it has been checked. SmartDocs will surface what changed, what was verified, and what could not be achieved.
